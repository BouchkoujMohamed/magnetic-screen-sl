Frequently asked questions

You might be wondering…

Will it fit my door?

It measures 100 × 210 cm, the size of most front and bedroom doors. Measure your door opening: it should be the same size or slightly smaller. For a double door, you'll need two.

Is installation really easy?

Yes. No drill: stick the Velcro strips onto the clean, dry frame, add a few pins, then press the curtain onto them. It takes just a few minutes.

What about children or pets?

That's exactly where it shines: they walk through without touching anything and the screen closes behind them. As with any mesh, avoid letting anyone hang on it or pull it.

Will it stop every mosquito in the house?

It protects the door it's fitted on. If windows are left open without protection, mosquitoes can still get in that way. Keep using your bed net and other malaria precautions too.

How do delivery and payment work?

After you order, we call you to confirm your address. Delivery is free in Freetown and the provinces. You pay the courier in cash when you receive the package.

Can I wash it?

Yes: unhook it from the Velcro and hand-wash it in warm soapy water without scrubbing hard, then let it dry in the shade before putting it back.
