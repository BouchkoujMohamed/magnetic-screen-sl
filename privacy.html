Who we are

What we collect when you order

Your full name, phone number and delivery address, and the package you choose (1 or 2 screens) with its price.

Why we use it

Only to handle your order: to call you to confirm it, to deliver it and to collect payment in cash on delivery. We don't sell your details and don't use them for anything else.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Orders are saved in our order spreadsheet (Google Sheets). Your details are seen only by the people who confirm and deliver your order.

How long we keep it

We keep your order details — your name, phone number, delivery address and what you ordered — for 12 months from the date of your order, unless a longer period is required by law. After that, we delete your name, phone number and address from our order records. We may keep the order number, date, package and price without your personal details, for our sales records.

Meta Pixel (advertising measurement)

This website uses the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms to measure our Facebook and Instagram advertising. It records that a browser visited the page, viewed the product, started filling in the order form and placed an order — with the package, quantity and price. Your name, phone number and address are not sent to Meta.

Meta may set cookies (such as _fbp) and use information about your browser and device under its own privacy policy. You can limit this by blocking third-party cookies or using an ad blocker — ordering still works — and you can manage your ad settings in your Facebook/Instagram ad preferences.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you gave us at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +212 727-788747.
